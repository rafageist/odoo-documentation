# Lead

## Definition
- A lead is an early-stage sales signal that has not yet been qualified into a real commercial opportunity.
- It usually represents uncertain demand, inbound interest, or a prospect that still needs triage.

## Why developers should care
- CRM customizations often fail when they assume every `crm.lead` record is already a mature sales opportunity.
- Requirements around qualification, assignment, scoring, and conversion usually live in the lead stage of the funnel.
