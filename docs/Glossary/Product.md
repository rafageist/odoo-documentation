# Product

## Definition
- A product is the sellable, purchasable, stockable, or service concept that the business works with.
- In Odoo, the business idea of a product usually maps first to the template layer rather than to one concrete SKU.

## Why developers should care
- Users say "product" loosely, but code often needs to distinguish between template-level behavior and variant-level behavior.
- Pricing, procurement, invoicing policy, routes, and ecommerce setup often start on the product template.
