# Warehouse

## Definition
- A warehouse is the operational stock location structure that represents where goods are stored and how they flow in and out.
- In business language, it is the site or facility that owns receiving, storage, and delivery processes.

## Why developers should care
- Stock routes, replenishment, picking flows, and operation defaults depend on warehouse structure.
- Users may say "this happens in warehouse B", but the implementation often involves warehouse, locations, routes, and operation types together.
